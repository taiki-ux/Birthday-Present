Dear Royda Kaputa,

Happy birthday! I wanted to give you something more than a normal message this year, so I made this. I couldn't wrap a website, but I tried my best.

I've known you for a very long time, and over the years you've become someone really close to me. You're family in a way that goes past the title. That's why I do not call you "mom".

You're funny, you talk a lot (in the best way), and you're one of the kindest people I know. You always try to make everyone around you laugh, and it works. Honestly, it's one of my favorite things about you.

And yes, I know. You "know nothing." I've heard it enough times to know it isn't true. You may not know everything, but you know how to make people feel happy and welcome, and that counts for a lot.

I know things haven't been easy lately with university thing. I won't pretend to have all the answers, but I do believe in you. You don't need your whole future figured out today. Take it one step at a time, and know I'm in your corner.

Thank you for being part of my life. I'm really glad you are. I hope your birthday is full of laughter and people who love you.

Happy Birthday, Royda. ❤️

With love,
Taiki
